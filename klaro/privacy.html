Klaro Privacy Policy

Klaro language-learning apps, including Klaro: Learn Bisaya. Published by Cognitive Products Lab. Last updated 25 October 2026.

The short version

Klaro does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your lesson progress, review schedule, streak, rewards and settings are stored only on your device.
  • All lessons, pictures and audio are part of the app, so using Klaro doesn't send anything to us.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

Backups you choose to make

If you tap Back up, Klaro creates a file with your progress and opens your phone's share menu. You decide where it goes (for example Google Drive, email or your files). We never receive it. Restoring reads a backup file you pick.

Feedback you choose to send

If you tap the flag to report a problem or send feedback, Klaro opens your email app with a message addressed to us. It includes your note and a description of the screen you were on (the lesson and the words shown), plus the app version and your chosen home region. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use these emails only to fix and improve Klaro, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

Purchases

If you buy the A2–B1 levels, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Klaro only learns whether the purchase is complete, so it can open those levels. We never see your card or payment details.

Children

Klaro is meant for teens and adults. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
