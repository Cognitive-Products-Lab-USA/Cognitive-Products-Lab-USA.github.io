Klaro: Learn Bisaya

An Android app by Cognitive Products Lab

Klaro teaches Bisaya (Cebuano), the language of the Visayas and Mindanao, with pictures, audio for every word, real dialogues and a short daily review that brings words back just before you'd forget them.

What's inside

  • Lessons from your first “Oo” to real conversations, levels A1 to B1.
  • Every word and sentence has audio, so you always hear how it sounds.
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • The beginner level (A1) is free. A2 and B1 are a single one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Get it

Klaro is in testing now and coming soon to Google Play.
