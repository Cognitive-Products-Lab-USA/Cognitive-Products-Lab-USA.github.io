Questions

Good to know

Is Bisaya the same as Cebuano?

Yes. Bisaya is what most speakers call it, and Cebuano is its formal name. Klaro teaches the everyday spoken language.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All lessons, pictures and audio are inside the app, so it works offline.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone, and you can make a backup file whenever you like.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Klaro is coming to Android first.

How do I get help or send feedback?
